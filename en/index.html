HOW IT WORKS

You do not need to know sake
before you walk in.

Tell us what you already like. We will start with a small pour, compare the differences and take it from there.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you like

    Wine, beer, whisky, something sweet — anything is a useful starting point.

  2. 02

    Start with one pour

    We will choose a small first glass using your preferences as a clue.

  3. 03

    Compare

    Try sake chilled, at room temperature or warm and notice what changes.

  4. 04

    Find the next one

    Tell us what worked — lighter, richer, more aromatic — and keep exploring.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to know much about sake?

No. Beginners are welcome. Tell us what you normally drink and we will start from there.

Can I come alone?

Absolutely. Solo guests are very welcome.

Do you speak English?

Yes. English-speaking staff are available, and this page covers the essentials in English.

Can I visit without a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome when seats are available. We recommend booking ahead for groups of three or more on Fridays.

How do I reserve?

Send your date, time and number of guests to @CHILLLABOTOKYO by Instagram DM. If you do not use Instagram, call us.

How can I pay?

Cash, major credit cards and PayPay are accepted.

Can I smoke inside?

No. The bar is non-smoking. There is a smoking area outside.
