Do I need to know about sake?

Not at all. Tell us about the drinks and flavors you enjoy, and we can help you explore. Beginners and solo guests are welcome.

How much does sake tasting cost?

The first hour is ¥3,300 per person, tax included. Extensions are automatic at ¥1,100 per additional hour. Food is ordered separately.

Is there a table charge?

There is no table charge with the tasting plan. For à la carte orders only, a table charge of ¥550 per person applies, including tax.

Is English support available?

English-speaking staff are available, and some menu information is in English. Please feel free to ask for help.

Can I walk in without a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome when seats are available. We recommend booking for Friday groups of three or more. For same-day reservations after 16:00 JST, please call.

Do you offer tasting courses with food?

Yes: a 2-hour course at ¥6,600 or a 3-hour course at ¥8,800 per person, tax included. Available for 2–10 guests, Monday–Friday, with booking at least 2 days ahead. For 10 guests, please call.

See the food courses ↓
Are other drinks available?

Shochu, beer and soft drinks are also included in the tasting plan. Sake is available chilled, at room temperature and warm.

Can I bring my own food?

Please do not bring outside food. We serve appetizers at the bar, with oden available Monday–Friday.

How do I get there?

We are about a 5-minute walk from either Akasaka Station or Akasaka-mitsuke Station. The bar is on 2F at 4-3-27 Akasaka, Minato-ku, Tokyo. See the alley photo and Google Maps for directions.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, credit cards and PayPay.

Is smoking allowed?

The bar is non-smoking indoors. A smoking area is available outside.

Can I buy a bottle on 1F?

Our 1F Bottle Shop is in preparation. Opening and sales details will be announced when confirmed.
