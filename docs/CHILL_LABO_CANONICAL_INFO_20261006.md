### 日本語一文
日本酒に詳しくなくても大丈夫。少しずつ飲み比べながら、あなたの「好き」を一緒に探す、赤坂の日本酒バーです。

### 日本語紹介文
日本酒に詳しくなくても大丈夫。100種類以上の日本酒を少しずつ飲み比べながら、スタッフと一緒にあなたの「好き」を探せる赤坂の日本酒バー。おでんなどの和食とともに、一人でもグループでも気軽に日本酒を楽しめます。

### English description
Chill Labo Akasaka is a sake bar in Akasaka, Tokyo, where you can discover the sake you truly like. You do not need to know anything about sake before you visit. Taste small pours from more than 100 varieties, compare different styles, and explore your preferences together with our staff. We also serve Japanese dishes such as oden that pair well with sake. Solo guests, groups, first-time sake drinkers, and international visitors are welcome, and an English menu is available.
